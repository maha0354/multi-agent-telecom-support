# Refunds

## Disputing a charge

If you believe a charge on your invoice is wrong, report it to customer service within 60 days
of the invoice date. You do not need to pay the disputed amount while the dispute is being
handled. Disputes are usually resolved within 14 days.

## Roaming charge refunds

Roaming pay-as-you-go charges are refunded if they were caused by a network fault on our side or
an incorrect zone classification. Charges caused by data use that you did not intend (for
example background app updates) are not refunded, but customer service may offer a one-time
goodwill credit.

## Travel Pass refunds

An unused Travel Pass can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. A pass that has started
(data has been used in the zone) cannot be refunded.

## Outage compensation

If a confirmed network outage stops your service for more than 24 hours in a row, you may
request a credit of one day's plan price for each full day without service. Request it from
customer service within 30 days of the outage.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are credited on your next invoice. If you have left Martins Mobile, the refund is
paid to your bank account.
