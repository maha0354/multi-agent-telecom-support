# Roaming

Martins Mobile is a fictional Swedish mobile operator. Roaming means using your phone on a
partner network outside Sweden. Every country we cover belongs to one roaming zone. Your plan
decides which zones are included; usage in other zones is charged per GB or covered by a
Travel Pass add-on.

## How roaming is charged

Data used in a zone included in your plan is taken from your normal data allowance. Data used in
a zone that is not included in your plan is charged per started 100 MB at the zone's
pay-as-you-go rate per GB, unless an active Travel Pass covers that zone. Calls and texts
abroad are not covered by this assistant; contact customer service for call rates.

Roaming is switched on by default for all plans. You receive a text message with the zone and
its rate when you arrive in a new country.

## Zone 1: EU and EEA

Countries: all EU member states, plus Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein.

Conditions: Zone 1 is included in every Martins Mobile plan under the EU "Roam Like at Home" rules.
A fair-use limit applies: data used in Zone 1 counts against your normal allowance, and
long-term use abroad (more than 4 months in a row with more use abroad than in Sweden) may
lead to a surcharge after a warning.

## Zone 2: Europe outside the EU

Countries: United Kingdom, Switzerland, Turkey, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, North Macedonia,
Albania and Montenegro.

Conditions: Zone 2 is included in plans that list Zone 2. Without it, data is charged at the
Zone 2 pay-as-you-go rate. Travel Passes for Zone 2 can be bought in the app.

## Zone 3: North America

Countries: United States, Canada and Mexico.

Conditions: Zone 3 is included only in plans that list Zone 3. Without it, data is charged at
the Zone 3 pay-as-you-go rate or covered by a Zone 3 Travel Pass.

## Zone 4: Asia-Pacific

Countries: Japan, South Korea, China, Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia,
Australia and New Zealand.

Conditions: Zone 4 is only included in plans that list Zone 4. In those plans a fair-use limit
of 20 GB per month applies in Zone 4; data above the limit is charged at the Zone 4
pay-as-you-go rate. Without Zone 4 in the plan, data is charged at the Zone 4 pay-as-you-go
rate or covered by a Zone 4 Travel Pass. Some networks in China restrict certain services
(for example Google services) regardless of plan.

## Countries outside all zones

Countries not listed in Zones 1 to 4 are not covered by Martins Mobile roaming agreements. Data may
not work at all there. Use local Wi-Fi or a local SIM card.

## Travel Pass add-ons

A Travel Pass is a prepaid data bundle for one zone. Conditions:

- It starts when you first use data in the zone, not when you buy it.
- It is valid for a fixed number of days, and unused data expires at the end.
- When the pass data runs out, the zone's pay-as-you-go rate applies until you buy another pass.
- A pass can be bought in the app or by texting PASS to 1234, up to 30 days before travel.
- Passes are not refundable once data use has started. An unused pass can be refunded within
  14 days of purchase (see the refund policy).

The available passes, their data amounts, durations and prices depend on the zone and can be
looked up for your account.
