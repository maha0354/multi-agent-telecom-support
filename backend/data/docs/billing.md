# Billing

## Billing cycle

Your billing month starts on the 1st of each month. The monthly plan price is charged in
advance; roaming pay-as-you-go charges and extra data are charged afterwards, on the next invoice.

## Invoices and payment

Invoices are sent by email and shown in the app around the 5th of each month, with payment
due on the last day of the month. You can pay by e-invoice, direct debit (autogiro) or card.

## Late payment

If an invoice is not paid on time, a reminder fee of 60 SEK is added. If it is still unpaid
30 days after the due date, outgoing calls and data may be blocked until payment is received.

## Understanding roaming charges on your invoice

Roaming pay-as-you-go data appears as a separate line per zone, showing the GB used and the
rate. Travel Passes appear as one line each on the invoice for the month they were bought.

## Spending limit

To avoid unexpected roaming charges, a default spending limit of 500 SEK per billing month
applies to roaming pay-as-you-go data. When it is reached, roaming data stops until you raise
the limit in the app or buy a Travel Pass.
