# Plans

Martins Mobile offers monthly mobile plans. The current plan list, with prices, data allowances and
included roaming zones, can be looked up for your account.

## What a plan includes

Every plan includes unlimited calls and texts within Sweden, a monthly data allowance, and
roaming in the zones listed for that plan. Zone 1 (EU and EEA) is included in all plans.

## Unused data

Unused data does not roll over to the next month on any plan.

## Changing plan

You can change plan in the app at any time. An upgrade takes effect immediately, and you pay
the difference for the rest of the current billing month. A downgrade takes effect from the
next billing month. There is no fee for changing plan, and plans have no binding period.

## Extra data

If you run out of data in Sweden, your speed is reduced to 1 Mbit/s until the next billing
month. You can buy extra data in the app; extra data is valid until the end of the billing month.
