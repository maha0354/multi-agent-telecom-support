# Customer Service Policy

## Contact channels

Customer service is available by chat in the app and by phone on 0771-123 456, weekdays
08:00 to 20:00 and weekends 10:00 to 16:00.

## What this assistant can and cannot do

This assistant can answer questions about plans, roaming, billing, refunds and connectivity,
and look up your own account and plan. It cannot change your plan, buy Travel Passes, issue
refunds, or change account details. For those, use the app or contact customer service.

## Privacy

The assistant can only access the account of the customer who is logged in. It never shares
information about other customers.

## Escalation

If a problem is not solved by the troubleshooting steps, or if a refund or dispute is needed,
contact customer service. Complaints that are not resolved can be escalated to a team leader,
and after that to the Swedish consumer agency for telecom (fictional reference for this demo).
